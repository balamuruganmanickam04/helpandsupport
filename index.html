How it works

Four steps from the problem to an app on your phone.

  1. Tell us the task

    Email us what you do by hand today and who does it.

  2. Get a plan

    We reply with what the app would do and how we would build it.

  3. Build and try

    You test early versions on your own phone and tell us what to change.

  4. Launch and support

    We help you install it and fix issues after it goes live.

Help and support

Quick answers. If yours is not here, email us.

How do I ask for a custom app?

Email us using the "Ask for an app" button below. Tell us what work you do by hand today, who would use the app, and when you need it. We will reply with questions or a plan.

What if I only have a rough idea?

That is fine. Describe the problem in your own words, even if you are not sure what the app should look like. We will help you shape it.

How do I report a problem with one of our apps?

Use the "Report a problem" button below and fill in the app name, your phone model, your Android version, and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Can I request a new feature for an app I use?

Yes. Email us what you would like the app to do and why. We read every suggestion.

How do I ask about my data or privacy?

Write to us at the email address below with the app name and your question. We will help you with it.
